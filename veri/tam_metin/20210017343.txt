Saffire-IV, Event 4: SIBAL Burn + 0.59g HCl Release

Why was HCl observed during the burn 
event but not the tracer gas release event? 

One possible explanation:

• HCl interacts strongly with aluminum 
and oxidized aluminum, so acid may 
be adsorbing onto cabin surfaces 
prior to reaching the FFD

• When HCl is released the second 
time, active sites on cabin surfaces are 
occupied from previous release, 
allowing more HCl to travel to the 
vehicle zenith

17

• Little CO removed by the smoke eater during Saffire-IV 
and V burn events

• Potential explanations:
• Catalyst fouling
• Bed packing integrity
• Concentrations of CO at the FFD are too low for 

the catalyst to exhibit substantial removal activity
• CO sensor problems – ongoing area of investigation

Conclusions

• Saffire-IV and V provided the first measurements of combustion 
products from large-scale fires in microgravity

• Key observations and areas for future work:
• Lack of acid gas at the FFD during scheduled releases → gas adsorption to 

cabin surfaces

• Particle mass concentrations were dominated by submicron particles

• Particle sizes and mass concentrations from combusting different fuels could be 
used in future modeling studies to generate smoke emission factors to better 
predict times to alarm for spacecraft smoke detectors

• While CO2 scrubber performed nominally during all scrub events, smoke eater 
did not remove CO as expected – still under investigation
