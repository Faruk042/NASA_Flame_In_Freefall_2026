heat release) in exploration atmospheres

 Evaluate fire behavior for a range of materials

technology on all three flights

Saffire IV & V Challenges

6

• Conduct mission simulations with remote operations
• Develop an alternate method to view NG MAESTRO screens (since the team couldn’t travel to Dulles, 

VA)

• Work with NG & NASA JSC to gain remote access to the Cygnus data due to Solarwinds breach
• Figure out how ZIN personnel will view Cygnus/Saffire data
• Conduct mission rehearsals with NG
• Identify means for Mission Ops Team members to have backup internet connectivity
• Ensure reliable communication between Saffire’s FOT-Console and NG and between FOT-Console and 

NG Systems Engineer 

• Get additional GSE laptops so more Mission Ops Team can run the Ground Application
• Set up servers at GRC so that data processing can be accomplished by computers inside the GRC 

firewall

• Develop method to maintain and communicate Operations Timeline



Solutions

7

• Successfully conducted mission simulations using remote access.
• MAESTRO screens were visible via streaming through WebEx (back-up)
• Data flow test was conducted and the Saffire data displays were accessible to the Saffire 

Mission Operations Team. Tied into Mission Support Environment (MSE) via JSC VPN

• ZIN personnel accessed the data via a shared display from MS Teams
• Rehearsal was successfully completed being 100% remote
• Smart phones with hotspot capability and/or MiFi hotspots were requested for all Mission 

Ops Team members for additional communication and back-up Wi-Fi

• NG set up an audio WebEx on the Ops1 loop so that FOT-Console could communicate with 
NG Mission Control. Telecon lines were used for communication between NG System 
Engineer and Saffire

• Obtained two additional GSE laptops that were configured to run the ground support 
software.

• Check-out and testing was done. This was a backup plan because we had approved access to 
GRC for Data Download - RTOW

• Operations Timeline Teams Channel



Lessons Learned

• Have backup plan for displaying telemetry
• Needed a backup method to communicate with the OPS1 loop
• Don’t have Ops start right after the holidays
• Besides emailing exact start times, also text
• Start Teams chat the day before
• Use the Teams Chat function to maintain another record of progress, issues, data, 

etc.

• Check the Ops Handbook for usability prior to Ops
• Simulations highlighted the susceptibility of the approach to transferring large files 

over home internet connections; get on-site access for data download

• More tool training
• More data download training
• Do equipment check prior to the mission

 The impact for spacecraft fire safety is that thin structures on a thick 
material can propagate a flame in zero-g even though the flame does 
not propagate on the thick material

· The test conditions are nominally at 
10.2 psia and 26.5 %O2. The flow is 20 

cm/s. The video is 10x actual speed

 About 5 seconds into the video, the 
igniter initiates a flame on the 
downstream 1-sided sample.

· For the wide samples, a flame travels 
along each edge of the rib

· When the flow speed decreases to 5 
cm/s about 38 seconds into the video, 
the yellow in the flames disappear

· At times you can see a second flame 
trailing the leading flame. This is the 
flame on the back side of the sample

· Note that the thinner ribs have the 
faster flame spread. Also note that the 
flame on the right-hand side of the 
sample doesn’t ignite the cross-stream 
ribs.
