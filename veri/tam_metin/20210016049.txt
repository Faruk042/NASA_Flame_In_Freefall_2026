Concluding Remarks

o An activation energy asymptotic model for steady cool flame propagation has been 
developed using a 5-step simplified chemistry for n-dodecane

o During cool flame propagation both the final flame temperature and the flame 
speed are controlled by chemical kinetics (unlike hot flame propagation)

o Comparison against 1-g experimental results at lower pressures and temperatures 
are shown to be reasonable

o The model can be extended to cases with ozone addition and compared against 
planar cool flame propagation experiments (e.g., Belmont’s work)

o Detailed comparison against experimental observations are underway
